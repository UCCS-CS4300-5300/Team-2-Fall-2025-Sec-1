What is Space Domain Awareness?

Space Domain Awareness (SDA) is the ability to detect, track, and understand objects and activities in orbit It ensures the safety, security, and sustainability of space operations by monitoring satellites, debris, and potential threats. OrbitStream brings SDA insights to your fingertips, offering a clear view of what’s happening above our planet.
